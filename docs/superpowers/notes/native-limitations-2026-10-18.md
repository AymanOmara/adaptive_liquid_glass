# Native limitations re-check, 2026-10-18

## Clear interactive press: measured improvement retained

`NativePressLight` now uses separate clear-glass light coefficients. Regular
remains unchanged. Clear light lift/glow: 0.0475/0.042; clear dark:
0.044/0.042; Gaussian sigma remains 41 pt. Flutter still owns the gesture
and drives the growth, stretch, and release hold.

Measured on the iPhone 17 Pro motion simulator, iOS 26.4, lossless 60 fps:

- Before: `build/motion/press-clear-native` (existing recordings).
- After: `build/motion/clear-light-calibrated` (fresh recordings).
- Both contain `light-fit.json`, generated by
  `tool/native/fit_press_light.py RUN --out RUN/light-fit.json`.
- Fresh capture command:
  `SKIP_BUILD=1 MODE=native tool/fidelity/record_motion.sh build/motion/clear-light-calibrated press-clear-capsule-photo`.

The fitter aligns the renderers at their independently detected onsets,
excludes an 8 pt rim, fits the mean held-frame residual at onset+18...27,
and validates on the disjoint onset+28...37 window. RGB interior validation
MAE on the photographed 200x56 capsule went from **6.858 to 6.251 light**
(8.8% lower) and **7.246 to 6.378 dark** (12.0% lower), out of 255. These
are held-frame interior figures, not the earlier regular whole-motion
metric. White light cannot remove the remaining refracted-edge and geometry
residual. Exact native touch-driven SwiftUI interaction remains unavailable
through supported APIs; see `native-touch-glow.md`.

Three synthetic fitter tests cover recovery, held-out pixels, overbright
baselines (negative corrections), and an empty interior. The widget test
checks that clear presses select the clear coefficients in both appearances.

## Native tab foreground: prototype rejected

A complete experimental glyph bridge was compiled and captured, then removed:
Flutter kept layout/semantics and rasterized monochrome tab content to cached
PNG alpha masks; SwiftUI drew them inside the glass; a separate capsule hole
followed the lens without changing the cached glyph. Colored custom icons
and badges stayed in Flutter.

`Rectangle.fill(.primary).mask(Image(...))` produced pale grey, low-contrast
glyphs: native untinted Music icon+label region MAE rose from 1.54 to 16.46
on white, 1.52 to 16.82 on black, and 2.66 to 15.98 on dark photo.
Semantic `Image(...).renderingMode(.template).foregroundStyle(.primary)`
restored crisp glyphs but kept primary/tint flat; the corresponding errors
were **1.71**, **2.41**, **3.31**, still worse than the existing Flutter
foreground. Moving a mask into SwiftUI does not itself grant UIKit TabView's
vibrancy.

Artifacts under `build/native-limitations`:

- `tabs-before`: installed prior build's native and SwiftUI reference stills.
- `tabs-prototype`: Rectangle/mask prototype stills.
- `tabs-template`: semantic template Image stills.
- `tab-label-prototype.png`: before | reference | Rectangle/mask prototype.
- `tab-label-template.png`: Rectangle/mask | reference | template Image.

Regions above are x=179...222, y=800...844 pt at 3x, RGB MAE. The light-photo
before bar had Apple's stale material appearance, so its whole-bar metric
is confounded and is deliberately not used as evidence for the bridge.

Review also identified requirements before any future bridge can ship:
stable per-mask image/revision caches on both sides, no per-frame PNG
transfer/decoding during a lens drag, stale raster/ack cancellation, clearing
native content on compositing fallback, preserving explicit color alpha,
view-local geometry under transforms, and delaying foreground takeover until
the native glass is visible. None of this speculative bridge remains in
production code.

The native lens still cannot sample the native bar as a Flutter shader
texture; the proposed glyph bridge did not address refraction of bar edges
or selective darkening of the overhang.

## Runtime appearance flip: hosting-controller replacement verified

Orderly terminate-and-launch captures (no overlapping app launches) are in
`build/native-limitations/flip-before`: `reference.png` (SwiftUI dark),
`dark.png` (Flutter native dark launch), and `flip.png` (native light launch
with `-flip 1`, captured after 4 seconds).

Over the exact capsule bounding box x=101...301, y=560...616 pt, RGB:

- Native dark launch versus SwiftUI dark: SSIM **0.99999966**, MAE **0.00021**.
- Runtime light-to-dark versus SwiftUI dark: SSIM **0.90125**, MAE **21.15**.

The existing container identity rebuild, UIKit style update and three-frame
settle reproduced the stale material above. Replacing the entire
`UIHostingController`, after applying the final model and with its UIKit
style set before attachment, fixes the measured failure. The replacement
is re-contained in the owning view controller and the existing three-frame
settle remains. Geometry-only updates keep the controller.

Fresh orderly captures in `build/native-limitations/flip-host-replacement`
use the same simulator, scene, crop and four-second settle:

- Runtime regular light-to-dark: SSIM **0.999999658**, MAE **0.000208**,
  equal to the direct dark launch.
- Regular dark-to-light: SSIM **0.999999722**, MAE **0.000129**.
- Clear light-to-dark: SSIM **0.999956975**, MAE **0.015351**.
- Clear dark-to-light: SSIM **0.999957188**, MAE **0.014990**.

`metrics.json` and `spot-checks.json` contain the scores; all images are
preserved in that directory. This pass retested the reproduced failing
scene and three appearance/variant spot checks, not the entire historical
72-scene flip sweep.

The required `example/integration_test/native_swiftui_test.dart` passed
**3/3** on iOS 26.4: native from the first frame; light-to-dark-to-light
rebuilds with stable geometry and settlement counts; containment, geometry
updates and disposal. Build and integration logs are
`/tmp/native-host-rebuild.log` and `/tmp/native-host-integration.log`.
Only after both visual comparisons and integration checks passed was the
obsolete runtime-flip bullet removed from README Known limitations.

## Complete system tab control: verified 2026-10-23

The rejected glyph-mask bridge above is not used. Stock two-to-four-tab
SF Symbol bars in native mode now host a complete `UITabBarController`.
Its labels, capsule and interactive lens share UIKit's own rendering and
real touch handling. Custom content/layout/material, search, minimizing,
plain-dot badges, Reduce Motion and older Flutter retain the existing path.

Flutter's newer public synchronous platform-view hit-test policy avoids
stale native recognizers reproduced with both older blocking policies.
The Dart public getter is probed dynamically, so the package still compiles
against its existing minimum Flutter SDK and falls back there. The plugin
registers public enum ABI value 2, only used when that newer API exists.
No private recognizer mutation or synthetic UITouch events are involved.

Measured on iPhone 17 Pro/iOS 26.4, Flutter 3.47.7:

- `build/native-limitations/system-tabs` holds white, black, light-photo
  and dark-photo SwiftUI/native stills. Whole visible bar RGB MAE is
  0.000057, 0.03713, 0.01742 and 0.02136 out of 255 respectively.
  The untinted Music symbol/label region has zero difference in all four.
- `build/native-limitations/system-tabs-direct-motion` holds real press,
  quick-tap and drag recordings with matched initial selections. Rest MAE
  is 0.0000, 0.00365 and 0.01947. Onset-aligned held frames 18–37 average
  0.5666, 0.1923 and 0.7685 respectively. Debug/JIT startup and external
  touch scheduling vary; these are image measurements, not latency claims.
  `metrics.json` preserves the frame counts and alignment offsets.
- Reproduction: `UDID=... SCENE=tabbarphotodark MODES=native SKIP_BUILD=1
  tool/fidelity/record_tabbar_motion.sh RUN press tap drag`, after building
  and installing the ordinary example app.
- `tool/native/check_tab_touches.py UDID` runs a normal app fixture through
  Flutter's runner and idb. It verified actual quick native taps, an opaque
  Flutter overlay receiving the tap while native selection stayed unchanged,
  recovery after removing the overlay, and real taps after a theme rebuild.
  The log and exposed/covered/rethemed screenshots are in
  `build/native-limitations/system-tabs-*`. The live WidgetTester harness
  did not deliver these UIKit taps reliably; it is not the source of this
  real-touch evidence.
- Native integration verifies containment, selection acceptance/rejection,
  theme replacement, invalid-symbol fallback/recovery, teardown and capsule
  extents for two/three/four tabs (including a long label). Widget tests
  cover reselect suppression, Flutter hit testing and pushed-route entrance
  and reverse transitions. Native creation waits until route entrance ends,
  rather than rejecting a temporary route transform permanently.

The README tab limitation was narrowed only after the normal-app real-touch
check passed. Custom-rendered tab differences and the generic shared-glass
press limitation remain documented.


### Bundled Flutter icon templates: verified after the SF path

The complete control also accepts ordinary Cupertino and Material IconData.
Only these bundled fonts are eligible; custom fonts, fallback-family lists,
widgets and plain-dot badges retain the existing renderer. A bounded per-bar
cache prepares normal/active white PNG alpha templates at display resolution.
UIImage uses the explicit scale and alwaysTemplate rendering. Native decoded
images are retained only for the current items and reused across selections.
There is no per-frame encoding, method-channel image transfer or item
rebuilding during native lens motion. Async results are discarded after disposal or newer
icon/DPR/direction changes; the Flutter bar stays usable while preparing them.

Evidence:
- Six helper tests include exact RGBA equality with a real rendered Flutter
  Cupertino Icon at 3x, active artwork, transparency and directional mirroring.
- Twenty final native-widget/helper tests pass; cache identity persists across
  selection, newer icon/DPR/direction wins an in-flight request, and disposal
  does not create a native view.
- Native integration sees 28 pt normal/selected template images, three decoded
  images throughout a selection change, unchanged native item revision, and
  zero retained tab controllers after disposal. All seven combined native /
  SwiftUI / capture regressions pass.
- `tool/fidelity/.venv/bin/python tool/native/check_tab_touches.py UDID --bitmap`
  exercises the public GlassTabBar with mixed Cupertino/Material icons over
  the reference photo. It asserts one real native host, quick native picks,
  a Flutter overlay receiving the blocked native tap, recovery, a held lens,
  and successful real taps after the appearance rebuild. Screenshots and logs:
  `build/native-limitations/system-tabs-bitmap-*`.
- Untinted Music label ROI x179–222/y830–844 pt has RGB MAE **0.0** against
  `system-tabs/tabbarphoto.swiftui.png`; `system-tabs-bitmap-metrics.json`
  records this. Cupertino/Material glyph shapes are intentionally retained,
  so this is not a claim that they become SF Symbols.
